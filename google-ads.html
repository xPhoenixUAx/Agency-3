BEFORE THE FIRST CAMPAIGN

Give every ad
a clear job.

A useful strategy connects what people are looking for to what your business needs next.

A commercial starting point
What are you selling, who is it for and what makes a customer valuable? We use your priorities to define the outcome the account should work towards.
A role for every campaign
Search captures existing intent. Product campaigns make the range easier to discover. Video and visual campaigns support discovery and return visits where they fit the journey.
A plan for useful signals
Before expanding the mix, we review what can be measured reliably and how leads, purchases or in-app actions will inform the next decision.

The output: campaign roles, audience priorities, a measurement plan and a proposed order of work — agreed around your starting point.

AFTER LAUNCH

Keep learning.
Keep the account moving.

Ongoing management turns campaign activity into a sequence of considered decisions. Priorities change as the account and your business develop.

01

Search intent

Review which queries attract interest, where the intent is weak and whether exclusions or more specific messaging are needed.

02

Account health

Check campaign delivery, product-feed issues, asset requirements and measurement changes that could affect decisions.

03

Testing priorities

Keep tests focused on a useful question: the offer, the audience, the product mix or the route to a conversion.

04

Business feedback

Bring sales-team input, product availability, margin context and lead quality into account reviews.
