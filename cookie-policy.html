What cookies are

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website asks your browser to store. When you return to that website or make another request, your browser can send the relevant cookie back. This helps a website connect related actions, maintain a session or remember a choice.

A first-party cookie is associated with the website you are visiting. A third-party cookie is associated with another provider. The distinction describes who sets or receives the cookie; it does not, by itself, explain its purpose or whether consent is required.

Cookie categories

Cookies can serve different purposes. The categories below explain the distinction; they do not mean that every category is active on this website.

  • Necessary cookies support a requested function, such as maintaining a secure form session. Our current form cookie belongs to this category.
  • Preference cookies remember choices such as a language or display preference. We do not currently set preference cookies.
  • Analytics cookies help measure how people use a website, for example which pages they visit. We do not currently set analytics cookies.
  • Advertising cookies support advertising measurement, audience creation or personalised advertising. We do not currently set advertising cookies.

Cookies we use

Our enquiry form uses the cookie described below. The form prepares its security session when the page containing it loads. Other pages can receive an existing session cookie from your browser, but do not require it simply to display their content.

How long cookies remain

Session cookies are intended for a browsing session. Persistent cookies have an expiry date and can remain for longer, unless you remove them first. We do not currently set persistent cookies on this website.

A browser cookie and information kept on a server have separate lifetimes. Removing the form cookie ends your browser's connection to that identifier, but does not necessarily erase related temporary security records immediately. Retention of enquiry information is addressed in our Privacy notice.

Similar technologies

Websites can also use browser storage, tracking pixels and other ways of storing or accessing information on a device. We do not currently use local storage or session storage to retain your form entries, preferences or advertising identifiers, and we do not use tracking pixels or browser fingerprinting.

Your browser may cache website files, such as images, fonts and styles, to load them more efficiently. Caching is separate from the form session cookie. The form's temporary server-side security records are also separate from cookies stored on your device.

Third-party services and external links

Advertising and video previews on this website are illustrative content. They are not embedded advertising networks or third-party video players, and viewing them does not load those services' tracking cookies.

If you follow a link to another website or open an email application, that service operates under its own privacy and cookie information. This policy does not describe or control the storage used by those services.

If we introduce an embedded player, analytics provider or other service that stores information on your device, we will update this policy to describe the provider, purpose and duration, and provide any choice required by applicable law before activating it.

Your choices and consent

Where applicable law requires consent for an optional cookie or similar technology, we will request it before using that technology. Simply reading this policy, scrolling a page or sending an enquiry is not treated as consent to optional advertising or analytics cookies.

There are currently no optional cookie categories to enable or disable through a website preference panel. You can control the necessary session cookie through your browser. If optional technologies are introduced, we will explain how to accept, reject or later change your choice. A later withdrawal would apply to future use.

Choosing not to allow optional cookies will not prevent you from reading our public pages or contacting us by email. Blocking the necessary form cookie can prevent the server from verifying or accepting an enquiry.

Managing cookies in your browser

Look for the privacy, cookies or site-data settings in your browser. Depending on the browser, you can inspect data for an individual website, remove existing cookies, restrict third-party cookies or block cookies more broadly. These settings normally apply separately to each browser, profile and device.

If you remove the session cookie while using the form, reload the page before making another attempt. If your browser prevents a new session from being created, you can contact us by email instead.

Private browsing can limit what your browser retains after a session, but does not make all online activity anonymous. For an independent introduction and links to browser-specific controls, see the Information Commissioner's Office guide to cookies.

Cookies and personal information

A cookie identifier may be personal information when it can be linked to an individual or their activity. The absence of your name in a cookie does not automatically make all related information anonymous.

The form cookie is used for session verification. Details you choose to send in an enquiry are handled separately. Our Privacy notice explains the operator's privacy practices and should be read alongside this policy. Contact us if you have a question about your information or wish to exercise a right available under applicable data protection law.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when website features, service providers or applicable requirements change. The date at the top identifies the most recent revision. A change to the text does not itself create consent to a new optional purpose; where a new choice is required, we will request it separately.
