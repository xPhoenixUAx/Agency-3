What this notice covers

This notice applies to visitors, prospective clients and people who correspond with us about our advertising, measurement and related services. It covers information received through this website, by email and during follow-up discussions about an enquiry.

If we provide services under a separate agreement, that agreement and any accompanying data processing terms may explain additional processing. When we handle a client's customer or campaign data on that client's instructions, the client's own privacy information may also apply.

Other websites and advertising platforms have their own privacy practices. A link or reference on this website does not extend this notice to services operated by someone else.

Information you provide

The audit form asks for your name, email address, website and the service or goal you want to discuss. It also records your acknowledgement of this notice. You may choose to include a message with further context about your business.

If you contact us directly, we receive the content of your correspondence and any information or attachments you choose to send. Follow-up discussions may include your role, business objectives, campaign context and the practical details needed to assess a potential engagement.

Please provide only information relevant to your request. Do not send passwords, payment card details, identity documents, sensitive personal information or customer lists through the general enquiry form. If account access or additional information is needed, we will discuss an appropriate way to provide it.

You are not required to submit an enquiry. If you choose to do so, the fields marked as required help us understand the request and respond; without them, we may be unable to process it. If you share another person's information, make sure you are authorised to do so and that they know it has been shared.

Technical information

When your browser connects to the website, the hosting infrastructure receives information needed to deliver the page. Depending on the server's operation, this can include an IP address, request time, requested URL, browser information and error or security records.

The enquiry form uses a session identifier and request checks to help prevent unauthorised submissions. Temporary security records help limit repeated requests. These records support the operation of the form; they are not intended to create advertising profiles.

The current website does not include advertising pixels or an analytics tracking service. The campaign previews are illustrations, rather than advertisements served to you by an advertising network. Cookie details and browser controls are explained in the separate Cookie Policy.

How we use information

We use information relevant to the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries: understanding what you need, answering questions and arranging an appropriate follow-up.
  • Preparing an audit or proposal: reviewing the website and campaign context you provide, assessing suitability and explaining possible next steps.
  • Managing a business relationship: maintaining relevant correspondence and, if you engage us, coordinating the work agreed with you.
  • Operating and protecting the website: delivering pages, investigating faults, preventing spam and responding to misuse.
  • Meeting obligations and handling disputes: keeping records required by law and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims where necessary.

We do not use the website form to make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Automated request checks may reject suspicious submissions; you can contact us by email if you have difficulty using the form.

Reasons for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we use the basis appropriate to the purpose and our relationship with you:

  • Steps towards or performance of a contract: responding to a request you make about entering an agreement with us, or carrying out an agreement with you.
  • Legitimate business interests: communicating with business representatives, assessing enquiries, maintaining relevant records and securing our website, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.
  • Legal obligations: keeping or providing information where an applicable legal requirement makes this necessary.
  • Consent: carrying out an optional activity where consent is required or is the basis we ask you to provide. You can withdraw that consent for future processing.

The acknowledgement beside the form confirms that you have been given this notice. It is not a general agreement to unrelated marketing or to every possible use of your information.

Who receives information

Enquiries are sent to our contact mailbox and may be accessed by authorised people who need the information to respond or support the work. The website enquiry flow does not automatically upload your message to an advertising platform or a customer relationship management system.

Service providers involved in website hosting, email delivery, storage and technical support may process information as part of providing those services. Where they act on our behalf, their access and use must be limited to the service they provide and subject to appropriate confidentiality and data protection arrangements.

Relevant information may also be shared with professional advisers or competent authorities when necessary for advice, a legal obligation, an investigation or the protection of rights. If the business is reorganised or transferred, relevant records may be considered or transferred subject to appropriate safeguards and any notices required by law.

We do not sell website enquiry information or give it to advertising networks for their independent marketing. We do not publish an enquiry, identify you as a client or use your business as a public case study merely because you have contacted us.

International processing

Website and email services may involve processing in a country other than the one in which you live. The location depends on the providers and arrangements used for the relevant service.

Where a transfer is subject to data protection restrictions, the applicable requirements must be met, for example through a recognised adequacy decision or appropriate contractual safeguards. You can contact us for information about the arrangements relevant to your data and how to obtain details of any applicable safeguards.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information for the period needed for the purpose for which it was collected, taking account of the type of record and any applicable legal requirements. Different records may therefore have different retention periods.

  • Enquiry correspondence: while we address your request and any related follow-up, with further retention only where needed for an ongoing business discussion, a record of our response or a relevant legal obligation or claim.
  • Client and transaction records: for the relationship and any additional period required for contractual, accounting, regulatory or legal purposes.
  • Technical and security records: for the operational and investigation needs they support, taking account of the server and provider retention arrangements.

Retention decisions consider whether the matter is still active, whether the information remains useful and proportionate, and whether a legal requirement or dispute justifies keeping it. Information that is no longer needed should be deleted or anonymised; backup copies may remain until they are replaced through the relevant backup cycle.

You can ask us about the retention period or criteria that apply to a particular record, or request deletion where applicable.

Keeping information secure

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the information and the risks involved. The website form includes request verification and controls against repeated submissions. Access to enquiry information should be restricted to people who need it for a relevant purpose.

No website, email service or internet connection can be guaranteed to be secure in every circumstance. Please take care with the information you send and let us know if you believe an enquiry or communication involving us has been compromised. Where a security incident requires a notification under applicable law, the relevant notification requirements apply.

Your rights and requests

Depending on the law that applies and the circumstances, you may be entitled to ask for access to your information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, a restriction on use or a portable copy of information you have provided. These rights can be subject to conditions and lawful exceptions.

You may also have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to object to direct marketing. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Send your request to . Explain which right you want to exercise and provide enough context to locate the relevant records. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity or authority, but you should not send identity documents unless we request them through an appropriate channel.

We will respond within the period required by applicable law. If a lawful exception, extension or permitted charge applies, we will explain it. Exercising a privacy right will not, by itself, result in discriminatory treatment.

Communications and marketing

We may contact you about the enquiry you send, including relevant questions, an audit discussion or a proposal. These communications relate to your request and do not automatically enrol you in a newsletter or a continuing promotional programme.

If we offer optional marketing communications, we will explain the choice and obtain consent where required. You can ask us to stop promotional messages by contacting us or using an unsubscribe facility where one is provided. We may still need to send messages about an active request, agreed services or legal matters.

Children's information

This website is intended for people enquiring about business services and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children through the enquiry form. If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, contact us so that we can review it and take suitable action.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the website, our services or relevant requirements change. The date at the top identifies the current version. Where a material change requires additional information, notice or consent, we will provide it in the manner required by applicable law.

Contact and complaints

You may also have the right to complain to a competent data protection or privacy authority, including an authority in the place where you live or work. Contacting us first does not remove or limit that right.
